About Me

Building a career in information technology

A short introduction to my background, the skills I am developing, and the kind of IT work I want to do.

Professional summary

I am Josiah Nzeki Mutisya, an intermediate IT professional based in Kenya and a Diploma in Information Technology graduate from KCA University. My interest in technology started with a simple curiosity about how systems work and grew into a desire to solve real problems with practical, well-thought-out solutions.

Between 2021 and 2026, I built a solid technical foundation at KCA University. My coursework covered programming methodology, network design and setup, principles of computer support and maintenance, and data structures and algorithms. The programme mixed theory with hands-on work, and it taught me to approach technical problems methodically: understand the issue, test a solution, and learn from the result.

In 2025, I spent three months as an IT intern at Tri Clover Industries Ltd in Nairobi. That was my first real experience of IT in a working business environment. I supported inventory control using Sage Inventory software, took part in stock-taking, assisted with network installation and configuration, and provided basic technical support to colleagues. The role showed me how the systems I had studied in class directly support day-to-day operations, and it taught me to explain technical matters clearly to people in other parts of the organisation.

Beyond technical skills, I bring a professional attitude. I communicate clearly, work well in a team, and approach problems with patience and persistence. I am comfortable asking questions when I do not know something, and I see continuous learning as a habit rather than a one-time event — a mindset that has already helped me grow from classroom knowledge into real-world practice.

My goal is to grow into a dependable IT support and systems professional. I am especially interested in roles where I can combine technical support, networking, and business systems to help an organisation run smoothly, while continuing to develop my skills and take on new responsibilities.
